04 | Digital Ethics

Be Smart, Safe, and Kind Online

What is Digital Ethics?

Digital ethics means using technology and the internet in a responsible and respectful way. As students, we should understand that our actions online can affect other people.

Five Rules for Responsible Internet Use

  • 01. Respect other people online.
  • 02. Do not spread fake news.
  • 03. Protect personal information.
  • 04. Give credit to original creators.
  • 05. Think before posting or sharing.
Use technology to spread knowledge, not hate. Be a light in every digital space.
